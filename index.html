Process

One sheet. Four steps. One pack.

  1. 01Structure

    Every pack starts as a dieline — cut, crease and glue lines engineered around your product.

  2. 02Print

    Offset for cartons, precision rotogravure for flexible packs: sharp registration on every sheet.

  3. 03Finish

    Hot-foil, embossing, drip-off UV and lamination, placed where the eye and hand land.

  4. 04Fold & glue

    Pre-glued, crash-lock and tuck-end formats that ship flat and pack fast.
